    Connecting to Anna…

    Three independent controls

    The model, policy engine and wallet have different jobs. No single component can bypass the others.

    01

    AI interprets the request

    Extracts amount, asset, network and recipient. It may flag risk or ambiguity. It cannot grant permission.

    02

    Verigate evaluates policy

    Deterministic rules return ALLOW, WARN or BLOCK. The signed Decision Receipt records the exact action and decision.

    03

    The wallet executes

    A separate one-time Execution Authorization binds the exact transfer. The wallet retains the private key and asks for the final signature.

    Important: ALLOW means the configured policy permits the request. It does not prove the recipient is safe, and it is not evidence that a transfer was sent or settled.
    If Anna's host model is unavailable, the app can show a clearly labelled policy-only preview. That degraded path cannot issue execution authority.
    RESULT

    Authority decision

    Anna AI contextual risk review (advisory)

    Source: bundled Verigate Executa → Anna protocol-v2 reverse Sampling. Findings are advisory; policy authority stays in Verigate.

      Policy limits

      The 24-hour counter tracks active policy reservations, not chain-confirmed spend. A verified signed REJECTED or ABORTED wallet outcome releases its reservation. UNKNOWN and SUBMITTED remain reserved until the outcome is resolved.
